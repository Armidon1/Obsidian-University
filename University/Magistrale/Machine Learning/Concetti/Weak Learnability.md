# Weak Learnability

> [!summary] In short
> A γ-weak learner returns, with probability ≥ 1−δ, a hypothesis with error at most 1/2 − γ: just slightly better than random guessing. Boosting turns weak learners into a strong one.

- $\gamma$ = advantage over a random classifier; $\delta$ = failure probability.
- Example: decision stumps (one-test trees) found by ERM.
