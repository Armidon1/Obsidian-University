# Union Bound

> [!summary] In short
> $P(A_1\cup\dots\cup A_k)\le\sum_iP(A_i)$: the probability that at least one bad event happens is at most the sum of their probabilities.

Used to pass from "each fixed $h$ is well estimated" to "**all** $h\in\mathcal{H}$ are well estimated", paying a factor $\lvert\mathcal{H}\rvert$.
