# Elastic Net

> [!summary] In short
> A convex combination of the L1 (Lasso) and L2 (Ridge) penalties, $\alpha\lVert\theta\rVert_1+(1-\alpha)\lVert\theta\rVert_2^2$.

- Gets sparsity from L1 and stability with correlated features from L2.
- Also usable as a regulariser for neural networks.
