# Clustering

> [!summary] In short
> Partition unlabelled data so that similar points are grouped and dissimilar ones separated; the two goals can conflict and there is no unique formalisation (no ground truth).

- Input: $(X,d)$ with $d$ a distance (zero on the diagonal, symmetric, triangle inequality).
- Output: partition $C_1,\dots,C_k$ or a **dendrogram**.
- Families: linkage-based (hierarchical), cost-minimisation (k-means), spectral…
