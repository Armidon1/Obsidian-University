# Guadagni e decibel

> [!summary] In breve
> $A_v=v_o/v_i$, $A_i=i_o/i_i$, $A_p=P_o/P_i$. In dB: $20\log_{10}|A_v|$, $20\log_{10}|A_i|$, $10\log_{10}A_p$.
